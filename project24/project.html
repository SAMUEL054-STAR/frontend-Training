How Math.random() works

Math.random() → a decimal: 0.0 to 0.999...
Math.random() * 100 → 0.0 to 99.9...
Math.floor(...) → rounds DOWN: 0 to 99
Math.floor(Math.random() * 100) + 1 → 1 to 100 ✅

The + 1 shifts the range so it starts at 1, not 0.
