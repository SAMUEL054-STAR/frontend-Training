The modulo operator %

number % 2 gives the remainder when dividing by 2.

10 ÷ 2 = 5, remainder 0 → Even
7 ÷ 2 = 3, remainder 1 → Odd

Same idea for other divisors: n % 3 === 0 means divisible by 3.
